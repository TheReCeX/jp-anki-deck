A vocabulary deck for Genki and Tobira, in German and English. It comes with a kanji popup, writing practice and more. This guide appears at the very start. When you are done, tap Got it · put away at the bottom. Then it will not come back. Later you can find it in the browser under the tag Anleitung.

The essentials in 30 seconds

  • Studying: deck Japanisch at the very top, the chapters come in order (How to study)
  • Two card types: Recognition (Japanese → meaning) and Recall (meaning → Japanese) (The cards)
  • Back: tap a kanji for the stroke order animation, info and words (Kanji popup)
  • Recall: “✎ Write word” to practise the whole word (Write word)
  • Slider icon top right: adjust language, furigana, rōmaji and colors (Settings)
  • New to Japanese? Still trouble with the kana readings? Turn on rōmaji for the start (Settings)

The cardsRecognition, recall, tapping kanji
  • Recognition: The front shows the Japanese. Tap “Show reading” to see the reading. The back shows reading and meaning.
  • Recall: The front shows the meaning. The back shows the Japanese with its reading. There is also “✎ Write word” here.
  • Tap a kanji: On the back, tapping a kanji opens the kanji popup with the animated stroke order. This does not work on the front of recognition cards. The popup would give away the meaning there.
  • Settings: The slider icon in the top right opens the settings. They apply to all cards and are saved on the device.
学
How to studyDeck, order, specials, import
  • Study via the deck Japanisch at the top. The chapters come in order. In the full deck: Genki 1, Genki 2, Tobira. Until then: the example chapters 01 to 04.
  • Every day both directions come mixed.
  • You can exclude extra vocabulary such as the specials: in the browser, search for tag:*::Special (for the kanji specials tag:*::Kanji-Special), select all cards (Ctrl/⌘+A) and suspend them (Ctrl/⌘+J).
  • When importing on the computer, tick “Import any deck presets”. Then the options are already set up: 20 new cards per day, second direction on a different day.

漢
Kanji popupStroke order, four modes, kanji info, radicals

Tap a kanji on the back. “Clickable kanji” has to be on for this (settings). The list below the picture explains the numbers.

  • 1Kanji bar: Switch between the kanji of the card at the top.
  • 2Modes (same in the word writing pad):
    • Watch: The stroke order plays as an animation.
    • Blind: You draw without help. Once all strokes are written, the original appears for comparison.
    • Snap: No template. Correct strokes snap in, wrong ones disappear. After three misses the right stroke flashes.
    • Guided: Like snap, but with a faint template. The next stroke is highlighted.
  • 3Stroke field: The stroke order plays here, or you write yourself. The practice is not graded. It has nothing to do with the Anki buttons.
  • 4Kanji info: Meaning in German or English, on and kun readings, radical with its Japanese name (e.g. ごんべん), parts, strokes, school grade, JLPT level and frequency.
    • School grade: It tells when Japanese children learn the kanji, following the current curriculum: 小1–小6 in elementary school, 中 for all other Jōyō kanji (they are added in junior high), 人名用 for kanji only allowed in names.
      • Role of senior high school: Japanese classes in senior high are not only about readings. Instead, the Jōyō kanji already learned are consolidated and deepened. Pupils also learn characters that are not Jōyō kanji (such as jinmeiyō kanji for personal names, or rarer, harder kanji and compounds), and widen their vocabulary in the context of classical and modern Japanese literature.
    • JLPT levels: The levels (N5–N1) are unofficial: there have been no official kanji lists since 2010. The levels come from Jonathan Waller and follow the old lists. Rare kanji have no level.
  • 5Tap the radical: The radical card opens. It shows name, reading and meaning. For many kanji it also shows the position, for example left as hen or top as kanmuri. It also lists variants and all kanji of the deck with this radical. Tap one of them: it opens on top (example). Use ✕, Esc or tap outside to get back to the radical card. This works over several levels.
  • 6Words with this kanji: Further down in the popup, below the kanji info. Words from the deck, grouped by reading. The word of the card is highlighted. A 書 stamp marks words you should be able to write. Each group first shows three words. Tap “more” to see the rest. In the full deck, all standard words from Genki and Tobira appear here. Right now these are the example words. Words you add yourself only appear here with the add-on.
  • For characters without stroke data only a note appears.
書
Write word (recall)Practise writing whole words

On recall cards, “✎ Write word” opens a writing pad for the whole word. It works on the front and the back and also for long expressions. What you write on the front is still there after turning the card.

The list below the picture explains the numbers.

  • 1Modes: As in the kanji popup. The difference: after watching, “Watch” moves on to the next character by itself.
    • Watch and guided are only available on the back, because they show the answer. Switching modes starts the word over.
    • Front side in blind mode: Nothing should be given away here. So the strip only grows when you tap “Next”. There is no counter and no punctuation. It also does not move on by stroke count. Instead of “Auto advance” there is a “Hint” button. It reveals the length, then the kana, then the template of the character. With “Kana given” the strip is complete as usual.
    • At the end: In blind mode on the back, the original appears faintly behind every character, in the strip too. What you wrote on the front shows up this way right after turning the card. In snap and guided mode the number of misses appears on each character.
  • 2Strip: All characters appear as small boxes at the top. Tap a box to jump to that character.
  • 3Writing field: Write the current character here. The stroke count of every character is known. So after the last stroke the pad moves straight to the next box. You can turn this off with “Auto advance”. Then you switch via the strip.
  • 4Buttons: “Stroke” undoes the last stroke. “Clear” empties the current character. “Again” starts the whole word over. In watch mode it restarts the animation at the first character.
  • 5Kana given: Kana such as ぶ in 学ぶ are already filled in. You only write the kanji. “Auto advance” skips the kana. You can still tap them and write them. Punctuation like 、 is always skipped.
  • The practice is not graded. It has nothing to do with the Anki buttons.
  • You can turn the feature off in the settings under “Write word (recall)”.
設
SettingsLanguage, furigana, rōmaji, 書 stamp
  • Language, appearance, accent color: Changes apply at once, even while a card is open.
  • Display width, sizes, font, alignment: The values are relative to the screen. This way the card fits phones and computers.
  • Rōmaji instead of furigana: For beginners. The reading appears in rōmaji above every character, kana included. It is off by default. On the front, rōmaji only appears after “Show reading”, just like the reading.
  • Furigana above kanji: Shows the reading in small print above the kanji.
  • Chapter tags: Shows on the back which book and chapter the word comes from.
  • Show reading on front immediately: Recognition only. The reading appears on the front right away.
  • 書 stamp: Marks the words you should be able to write according to the Japanese Studies curriculum at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg. In the full deck these are the words from the Genki kanji lessons, the Tobira words tagged 漢字RW and the words tagged Radikal漢字. On recall cards the stamp sits in the top left. In the word list of the popup it appears faintly in the background. You can turn each one off separately.
  • Greyed-out options depend on another option. For example, furigana is greyed out while rōmaji is on.

拡
Add-on for the computer (optional)Kanji word index (optional)

The word list in the popup is stored in the deck as the file _kvw.js. It contains all words of the deck. With the add-on Kanji Word Index, words you add yourself appear as well.

  • Install: Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons, code 1607352118. Then restart Anki.
  • AnkiWeb page: ankiweb.net/shared/info/1607352118
  • The add-on updates the list on startup, before every sync and shortly after changes. To do it by hand, use Tools → Update kanji word index now.
  • The add-on is also needed for the button Got it · put away at the end of this guide. Without it: press @.
  • Phones do not need an add-on. The file arrives via sync.
  • Words you only add on the phone appear after the next sync from the computer.
限
LimitsWhat does not work
  • Rōmaji is generated automatically. A rule detects the particles は and へ. In rare cases it gets them wrong.
  • Some kanji have no stroke data. These are only rarer kanji, for example variants like 麵.
  • The Reading field must use the furigana format. Otherwise the readings are not matched correctly.
  • The German kanji meanings are machine-translated from English. For very rare kanji even the English source is sometimes unclear.
